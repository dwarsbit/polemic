---
title: Introduction
description: Polemic is an open source, local-first LaTeX editor for the desktop.
---

Polemic gives you a code editor and a live PDF preview, and compiles everything on your
own machine with your own TeX distribution. Think of it as a lightweight desktop
alternative to Overleaf: no account, no upload, no cloud. Your project is a plain folder
on your disk.

<Callout type="warn" title="Pre-alpha">
  Polemic is under active development. Things work, but expect rough edges and
  changes between versions.
</Callout>

## What you get

- **Two editor faces.** A [code editor](/docs/writing/code-editor) with LaTeX-aware
  completion, and a [visual editor](/docs/writing/visual-editor) that shows the
  document as rendered content.
- **Live PDF preview** with SyncTeX: click in the source to jump to the PDF, and back.
- **Bibliography tools** for BibTeX files and Zotero, with citation hygiene built in.
- **An assets panel** for images and PDFs, with ready-made `\includegraphics` inserts.
- **Comments, snapshots and git** for reviewing and versioning your writing.
- **An optional AI quickfix** for compile errors the built-in rules cannot fix.
