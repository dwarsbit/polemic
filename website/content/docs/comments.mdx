---
title: Comments
description: Leave review notes on your text without touching the LaTeX.
---

Comments attach to a passage of text and live outside your source files, in
`.polemic/comments.json`. Your `.tex` files stay free of review notes.

Each comment has a category, shown as a colored bar in the editor gutter:

| Category       | Use it for                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Change needed  | Something to rewrite or fix          |
| Source needed  | A claim that needs a citation        |
| Question       | Something to find out or discuss     |
| Wording        | Phrasing to improve                  |
| Verify         | A fact or number to double-check     |
| Note           | Anything else                        |

Click a bar to open the comment. The **Comments** tab in the right rail lists them all;
resolved comments fade but stay available. Comments follow their text when you edit
around them.
