---
title: Assistant
description: Get help with LaTeX errors, packages, tables and layout from an AI model you choose.
---

The assistant is a chat beside your document. It reads your project, checks the compile
issues and proposes changes as diffs. Nothing is written to a file until you apply a
change. It is off until you set up an AI provider.

## Set up a provider

Open **Settings → Assistant** and choose a provider:

- **Mistral**, **OpenAI** or **Anthropic**: paste an API key from your account with them.
  Polemic loads the models your key can use; pick one that supports tool calls.
- **Ollama** or **LM Studio**: a model on your own machine, with no key. Polemic lists
  the models you have pulled.
- **Custom**: any other OpenAI-compatible API. Enter its base URL up to `/v1`, a key if it
  needs one, and the model name.

Polemic calls `<base URL>/chat/completions`.

## Ask a question

Open **Assistant** in the right sidebar and type your question, for example “This table
runs into the margin. How do I make it fit?”. You can also:

- right-click in the editor and choose **Ask the assistant about the selection**, to send
  the selected lines with your question;
- choose **Ask AI** on an issue in the issues list, to ask for a fix for that error.

While it works, the assistant shows what it does, such as the files it reads. Choose
**Stop** to end it.

## Review changes

Each proposed change shows as a diff with **Apply** and **Discard**. An applied change
lands in the editor and undoes like any other edit.

## Choose what it helps with

The menu at the top of the panel sets the mode:

- **LaTeX**: syntax, errors, packages, tables, floats, math and layout. It leaves your
  text alone, and a change that edits your words shows a warning.
- **Prose**: wording, clarity and structure of your text.
- **Both**: LaTeX and your text.

## Compiling

To check a fix, the assistant can compile your project. The first time, it asks for your
permission for that project. It saves your open files before it compiles. To take the
permission back, turn off **Allow compiling** in the panel's menu.

## Local models

Choose **Ollama** or **LM Studio** as the provider to run a model on your own machine.
Then nothing leaves your computer. For another local server, choose **Custom** and point
the base URL at it, for example `http://localhost:8080/v1`.

<Callout type="warn" title="HTTPS only">
  Remote base URLs must use `https`. Plain `http` is accepted only for `localhost`.
</Callout>

## What is sent

The assistant sends your question and the parts of your project it reads (files, compile
issues) to the provider you set up. The API key is stored in your system's credential
store and sent only to that provider.

The mode picker and the model sit under the question field: the model name opens the
settings.
