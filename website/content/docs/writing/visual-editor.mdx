---
title: Visual editor
description: Edit the document as rendered content while Polemic writes the LaTeX.
---

The visual editor shows your `.tex` file as a document: headings, lists, math and
figures look like what they are. Polemic regenerates the LaTeX behind the scenes and
keeps anything it does not model exactly as you wrote it.

Switch between the **Code** and **Visual** faces with the face toggle in the editor. Your
cursor keeps its place when you switch.

## What is modeled

- Sections and headings, with a level dropdown in the toolbar. Type `#` to
  `#####` at the start of a line for a heading.
- Bold, emphasis and underline, bulleted and numbered lists, including `\item[label]`.
- Inline and display math, rendered as you write. Click to edit the source.
- Figures with thumbnails, footnotes as inline pills.
- `\cite`, `\ref` and `\label` as pills. Typed commands turn into pills on the closing
  brace. Cite pills warn about keys no `.bib` file defines; ref pills warn about
  undefined labels.
- `quote`, `quotation`, `center` and `abstract` blocks, and the theorem family
  (including your own `\newtheorem` environments) with editable titles.
- The preamble as a summary card, and `\maketitle` as an editable title card.

## What stays raw

Everything else, such as `verbatim`, TikZ and unknown commands, shows as a raw block
and round-trips byte for byte. Switching to the visual editor never rewrites LaTeX it
does not understand.

<Callout title="Document settings">
  The document settings dialog edits the class, font size, paper, columns, draft mode
  and title metadata without touching the preamble by hand.
</Callout>
